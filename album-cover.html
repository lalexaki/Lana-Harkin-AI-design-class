01 / Album cover study / 2026

A sound.
A signal.

AM — Arctic Monkeys

A cover study for “Do I Wanna Know?” that translates its repeating pulse into a spare black-and-white waveform. The process moves from broad mood prompts to precise control of amplitude, spacing, and typography.

Final cover study Open artwork to view full size ↗

From mood to measure

The design process

Nocturnal.
Tense. Hypnotic.

The starting point was a single white amplitude-modulation waveform on a flat black field. Generous negative space keeps attention on the signal, echoing the song’s restrained, repeating energy.

Later prompts described the location of the peaks, the scale of the central wave, and the curvature between them. A waveform reference helped turn a general aesthetic direction into specific visual instructions.

The final decisions

Let the line
do the talking.

White-on-black contrast, two dominant peaks, and a smaller central rhythm hold the composition together. The artist name and album title sit in the quiet upper-left area, separate from the waveform.

The most useful refinement was moving from “minimal” and “centered” to measurable placement and proportion. The final typography is preserved exactly as it appears in the supplied process document.
