✦ LANA HARKIN WEEK 2 · PLAYABLE FIRST DRAFT
A PLANET IN PROGRESS

Made for wandering.

A cozy 3D exploration draft by Lana Harkin, developed with AI assistance from her Cosmic Daydreamer proposal. The original Week 1 pitch described 2D; this Week 2 draft develops the requested 3D direction.

Original procedural 3D models, illustrated sky effects, and synthesized audio are created in code for this game. The supplied mood images inform the color, atmosphere, and dream logic; they are not embedded game assets.

Built with Three.js (MIT; license) and browser audio. No account, installation, or sound is required.

Build notes, rules, checks & next steps →

First-draft limitations: one compact island and one dream room; no saved progress between visits. Desktop keyboard/mouse and touch controls are supported. A browser with WebGL2 is required.
